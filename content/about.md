## About This Site

The home page is BITSTREAM, a brick-breaker about keeping files alive. The power-ups are named after the things that keep digital collections alive (LOCKSS, cold storage, format migration) and the things that destroy them (bit rot, link rot, the data deluge). The game was imagined by me and build with Claude because
<ol>
<li>I don't have the technical chops to undertake something like this, tbh. Coding JS just isn't my strong suit. And,</li>
<li>Even if I did have the chops to do it manually, that would take way more time than I have (see 2 kids above)</li>
</ol>

So Claude helps me bring a cool idea to life. I hope you enjoy it.
